Jana Armouti

Jana is a PhD student in Computer Science at Cornell Tech, working on machine learning for medical imaging. She received her B.Sc. and M.Sc. in Electrical and Computer Engineering from Carnegie Mellon University, where she worked on representation learning for 3D vision and biomedical data.

Research Interests

  • Representation learning
  • Physics-informed machine learning
